COVERED CAUSES OF LOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Risks of direct physical loss of or damage to Covered Property at the project site, in transit or in
temporary storage, except as excluded. Perils contemplated include: fire and lightning; explosion; windstorm
and hail; tornado; riot and civil commotion; vandalism and malicious mischief; theft and burglary; aircraft
and vehicle impact; falling objects and dropped loads; collapse of any part of the structure during erection
including collapse caused by the weight of construction materials or by shoring or formwork failure;
accidental discharge of water from temporary or permanent plumbing, fire protection or process systems;
rain and water intrusion into an incomplete structure; smoke; settling and subsidence caused by an insured
peril; damage during hoisting, rigging, lifting and setting; and damage during testing and commissioning
as endorsed.

EXCLUSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This policy does not cover:
  1.  Loss or damage caused by faulty, inadequate or defective design, plan, specification, survey,
      workmanship, construction material or method of construction. This exclusion applies to the cost of
      making good the defective work, material or design itself. However, if defective work, materials or
      design results in physical damage from a Covered Cause of Loss to otherwise sound property, that
      resulting damage is insured. (LEG 2/96 Defects Wording - End. PIN-DEF 305)
  2.  The cost of correcting, redoing, replacing or repairing work that does not conform to the contract
      documents or that fails to pass inspection, absent physical damage from a Covered Cause of Loss.
  3.  Loss resulting from delay, loss of market, loss of use, loss of rent, liquidated damages or penalties
      for non-completion, except as provided under the Soft Costs and Delay in Completion endorsement.
  4.  Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion, dampness or dryness of atmosphere, extremes of
      temperature, marring, scratching or denting, unless resulting from a Covered Cause of Loss.
  5.  Mysterious disappearance, unexplained shortage, or shortage disclosed on taking inventory.
  6.  Theft from an unattended vehicle unless the vehicle was locked and there are visible signs of forced
      entry, or the property was in a locked and secured enclosed body.
  7.  Dishonest or criminal acts by any insured, any employee, or any subcontractor or its employees,
      whether acting alone or in collusion.
  8.  Loss to any part of the project after it has been occupied or put to its intended use, or after the
      issuance of a certificate of occupancy or temporary certificate of occupancy for that portion, except
      where partial occupancy has been declared to and accepted by us in writing (see Partial Occupancy
      condition below).
  9.  Loss to existing structures, existing improvements or the land itself.
  10. Mould, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, or any resulting remediation cost, except to the extent of
      $ 100,000 annual aggregate where directly resulting from an insured water damage event that was
      reported within 72 hours of the water event.
  11. Contractors' tools, machinery, mobile equipment, vehicles, and equipment owned, leased, rented or
      borrowed by any contractor or subcontractor, and not intended to become a permanent part of the
      project. (Such property should be insured under a contractors equipment floater.)
  12. Loss caused by the failure to take all reasonable and necessary precautions to protect the property
      from an approaching or forecast storm, flood or freeze event, including failure to secure loose
      materials, dry-in exposed openings or drain water lines.
  13. Earth movement and flood, unless specifically endorsed to this policy.
  14. Penalties, fines, and the cost of any permit, approval or inspection not directly caused by a covered
      loss.
  15. War, terrorism (unless elected), nuclear hazard, communicable disease, and cyber incident.
  16. Loss occurring after the policy expiry date, after final acceptance of the project by the owner, or
      after this policy has been cancelled, whichever occurs first, notwithstanding any extension of the
      construction schedule not declared to and accepted by us.

SITE PROTECTION WARRANTY (PIN-PROT 610)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

As a condition precedent to coverage, the Insureds warrant that throughout the policy period:
  (a)  The project site is enclosed by a minimum six-foot chain link perimeter fence with lockable gates,
       secured outside working hours;
  (b)  Site lighting and a monitored construction camera system, or a nightly security patrol logged in
       writing, are maintained from the commencement of vertical construction;
  (c)  A hot work permit programme meeting NFPA 51B is enforced for all cutting, welding, torching, roofing
       and soldering, including a fire watch during the work and for sixty (60) minutes afterward;
  (d)  Portable fire extinguishers are provided on every floor and standpipes are extended as the structure
       rises, in accordance with NFPA 241;
  (e)  A written Water Damage Prevention Plan is in force, requiring that pressurised water lines be capped
       or isolated outside working hours, that a designated water shut-off attendant be identified, and that
       all wet testing be conducted only during working hours with personnel present;
  (f)  Prior to any forecast named storm or freeze event, loose materials are secured, openings are covered
       or dried-in and water lines are drained where practicable.

    practicable and in no case later than seventy-two (72) hours after discovery. Notice must be given by
    the First Named Insured or by the General Contractor and must state the project name, the policy number,
    the date and time of the occurrence, the parties involved and a preliminary description of the damage.
        Pinnacle Inland Claims - Construction Unit
        constructionclaims@pinnacleinland.example   |   (855) 555-0619 (24 hour)
        Online FNOL: fnol.pinnacleinland.example
2.  POLICE NOTIFICATION. Any theft, vandalism or malicious mischief loss must be reported to the local law
    enforcement agency within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery and the report number provided to us.
3.  PROTECTION AND MITIGATION. The Insureds must take all reasonable steps to protect the property from
    further damage, including immediate dry-in, water extraction, dehumidification and temporary shoring.
    Emergency mitigation expense up to $ 50,000 may be incurred without our prior approval.
4.  PRESERVATION OF EVIDENCE. Damaged materials, failed components, formwork, rigging and lifting gear must
    be preserved and made available for inspection. Photographic and video documentation must be taken
    before removal or repair.
5.  PROOF OF LOSS. A signed and sworn Proof of Loss must be submitted within ninety (90) days of our request.
6.  PARTIAL OCCUPANCY. No portion of the project may be occupied or put to its intended use without our
    prior written consent. Occupancy without consent suspends coverage for the occupied portion.
7.  SCHEDULE EXTENSIONS. Any extension of the construction schedule beyond the policy expiry date must be
    requested in writing not less than thirty (30) days before expiry. Extensions are subject to additional
    premium and are not automatic.
8.  OTHER INSURANCE. This policy is primary with respect to the insured project. Any contractors equipment
    floater, general liability policy or professional liability policy is not contributory with this policy.
9.  SUIT LIMITATION. Any action must be brought within two (2) years of the date of loss.
10. PREMIUM. Policy premium $ 214,600 for the 18 month term, fully earned at inception as to 30%.
    Rate: $ 0.327 per $ 100 of completed value.
