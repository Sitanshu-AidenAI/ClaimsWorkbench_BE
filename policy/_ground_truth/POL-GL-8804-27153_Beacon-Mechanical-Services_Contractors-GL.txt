COVERAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

COVERAGE A - BODILY INJURY AND PROPERTY DAMAGE LIABILITY. We pay sums the insured becomes legally obligated
to pay as damages because of bodily injury or property damage caused by an occurrence in the coverage
territory during the policy period, and we have the right and duty to defend suits seeking such damages.
Property damage caused by water escaping from piping, fittings, valves, coils, condensate systems, hydronic
systems or fixtures installed, serviced, replaced or worked upon by the insured is covered as an occurrence,
subject to the Water Damage Loss Control Condition and to the "your work" exclusion as modified by the
subcontractor exception.

COVERAGE B - PERSONAL AND ADVERTISING INJURY LIABILITY.
COVERAGE C - MEDICAL PAYMENTS - expenses incurred and reported within one year of the accident.
SUPPLEMENTARY PAYMENTS - defense expenses, bail bonds to $ 2,500, loss of earnings to $ 500 per day, court
costs, and pre- and post-judgment interest, all in addition to the limits.

EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In addition to the standard Coverage A exclusions (expected or intended injury; contractual liability other
than insured contracts; liquor; workers compensation; employer's liability; pollution; aircraft, auto and
watercraft; war; damage to property owned, rented, occupied or in the care, custody or control of the
insured; damage to that particular part of real property on which operations are being performed; damage to
"your product"; damage to "your work" subject to the subcontractor exception; impaired property; recall;
electronic data; and statutory distribution of material), the following apply:

  1.  PROFESSIONAL SERVICES EXCLUSION (FRM-PROF 410). No coverage for liability arising out of the
      preparation, approval or failure to prepare or approve any design, drawing, specification, load
      calculation, equipment selection, energy model or engineering report, or the provision of design-build
      or design-assist services. Coverage is written back for the ordinary means and methods of installation
      performed by the insured's own forces.
  2.  ABSOLUTE MOULD, FUNGI AND MICROBIAL MATTER EXCLUSION (FRM-MOLD 420). This exclusion applies whether or
      not the mould, fungi or microbial matter results from an otherwise covered water event.
  3.  ROOFING AND HOT WORK LIMITATION (FRM-ROOF 425). No coverage for property damage caused by fire arising
      out of any torch, open flame, kettle, heat gun or other hot work performed by the insured on or within
      three (3) feet of any roof surface, unless a written hot work permit was issued, a fire watch was
      posted during the work and for sixty (60) minutes afterward, and a final inspection was documented.
  4.  REFRIGERANT RELEASE LIMITATION (FRM-REF 430). Property damage and clean-up arising from the release of
      refrigerant is sublimited to $ 250,000 per occurrence and $ 500,000 aggregate.
  5.  WATER DAMAGE LOSS CONTROL CONDITION (FRM-WTR 435). It is a condition of coverage that: (a) all
      pressure testing of hydronic, chilled water, domestic water and fire protection piping installed by the
      insured is performed only during working hours with the insured's personnel present and with the
      building water supply attended; (b) systems are not left pressurised and unattended overnight or over a
      weekend unless leak-detection with automatic shut-off is installed and operational; (c) a written test
      record is completed and signed for each system; and (d) the general contractor is notified in writing
      before any system is filled or charged. Where the insured fails to comply, the self-insured retention
      applicable to any resulting water damage claim is increased to $ 100,000.
  6.  RESIDENTIAL AND HABITATIONAL LIMITATION (FRM-RES 440). No coverage for new construction of
      for-sale single family dwellings, townhomes or condominiums; service, repair, replacement and
      tenant-improvement work in habitational occupancies is covered.
  7.  SUBSIDENCE AND EARTH MOVEMENT arising out of trenching, excavation or backfill performed by the
      insured (FRM-SUB 445).
  8.  SILICA, ASBESTOS and LEAD (FRM-SIL 450).
  9.  COMMUNICABLE DISEASE (FRM-CD 455), CYBER AND DATA LIABILITY (FRM-CYB 460), and
      EMPLOYMENT-RELATED PRACTICES (FRM-EPL 415).
  10. CROSS-SUITS. No coverage for claims made by one insured against another insured, except for a claim
      arising out of bodily injury to an employee of one insured brought against another insured.
