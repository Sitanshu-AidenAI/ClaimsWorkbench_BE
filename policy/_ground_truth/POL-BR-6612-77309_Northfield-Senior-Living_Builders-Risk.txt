COVERED CAUSES OF LOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All risks of direct physical loss of or damage to insured project property while at the project site, in
transit, or at a declared or undeclared off-site storage location, except as excluded. Perils contemplated
include fire, lightning, explosion, windstorm, hail, tornado, weight of snow, ice or sleet, ice damming,
freeze and frozen pipe (subject to the Freeze Warranty), riot and civil commotion, vandalism, malicious
mischief, theft and burglary, vehicle and aircraft impact, dropped loads and crane or rigging failure,
collapse during erection, accidental discharge of water from temporary or permanent systems, rain and
snowmelt intrusion into an incomplete structure, smoke, and sudden and accidental damage arising from
testing and commissioning.

      is insured (LEG 2/96 wording, End. GLC-DEF 305).
  2.  Rework, correction, and the cost of remedying work that fails inspection or does not conform to the

  7.  Mysterious disappearance, unexplained shortage and inventory shortage.

  10. Flood, surface water, and water that backs up from sewers or drains.
  11. Earth movement, earthquake, subsidence, settling and soil compaction, except settling directly caused

  12. Loss caused by or resulting from failure to comply with any warranty stated in this policy, including
      the Freeze Warranty and the Site Security Warranty.
  13. Mould, fungus, wet rot, dry rot and bacteria, except as sublimited.
  14. Damage to the existing structure from any cause other than a Covered Cause of Loss, and any

  15. Asbestos, lead paint and hazardous material abatement, testing, handling and disposal, whether or not
      discovered as a result of a covered loss.
  16. War, terrorism (unless elected), nuclear hazard, communicable disease and cyber incident.

FREEZE WARRANTY AND SITE SECURITY WARRANTY (End. GLC-WAR 615)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FREEZE WARRANTY. From November 1 through April 15 of each year during the policy period, the Insureds
warrant that: (a) temporary heat will be maintained at a minimum of 45 degrees F in all enclosed areas
containing water-filled piping, or all such piping will be drained and blown out; (b) a daily log of
temporary heat operation and interior temperatures will be kept and made available to us; (c) a designated
individual will inspect the site each non-working day and after any interruption of temporary heat or
electrical power exceeding four (4) hours; and (d) any interruption of temporary heat exceeding twelve (12)
hours will be reported to us. Coverage for freeze and frozen pipe damage is suspended during any period of
non-compliance.

SITE SECURITY WARRANTY. The site will be fully fenced with lockable gates, illuminated, and monitored by a
recorded camera system with off-site storage of footage, or patrolled nightly with a written log, from the
commencement of vertical construction. High-value materials (copper wire, HVAC condensing units, appliances,
plumbing fixtures) will be stored in a locked container or a secured interior room until installation.

CONDITIONS AND CLAIM REPORTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.  NOTICE. Notice of any loss or occurrence must be given as soon as practicable and within five (5)
    business days of discovery. Notice may be given by any Named Insured, by the General Contractor, or by
    the producer of record, and should state the project name and address, the policy number if known, the
    date and time of loss, and a description of the damage.
        Great Lakes Construction Underwriters - Claims
        buildersrisk.claims@cascadiamutual.example   |   (800) 555-0288, option 3
2.  LAW ENFORCEMENT. Theft, vandalism and malicious mischief must be reported to the police within
    twenty-four (24) hours of discovery; a copy of the report must be furnished to us.
3.  MITIGATION. The Insureds must take immediate steps to prevent further damage. Emergency water
    extraction, drying, temporary heat, shoring, tarping and board-up up to $ 25,000 may be incurred without
    prior approval. Moisture readings must be documented daily during any drying operation.
4.  DOCUMENTATION. On request the Insureds must provide the contract documents, the current schedule of
    values, pay applications, the daily jobsite reports for the seven days before the loss, the temporary
    heat log, subcontractor certificates of insurance, and all photographs and video of the loss site.
5.  SUBROGATION AND WAIVER. This policy recognises the waiver of subrogation contained in Article 11 of the
    AIA A133-2019 contract as between the Owner, the Construction Manager and the subcontractors, to the
    extent of insured loss. We retain subrogation rights against manufacturers, suppliers, design
    professionals and any party not a Named Insured.
6.  PROOF OF LOSS within ninety (90) days of request. SUIT LIMITATION: two (2) years from date of loss.
7.  SCHEDULE. Any extension of the construction schedule beyond 02/01/2027 must be requested at least
    thirty (30) days in advance and is subject to additional premium.
8.  PREMIUM. $ 178,300 for the 24 month term. Rate $ 0.406 per $ 100 of completed value.
