COVERED CAUSES OF LOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All risks of direct physical loss of or damage to covered property, except as excluded, while at a jobsite,
at the Insured's premises, at a declared or undeclared temporary storage location, or in transit within the
United States and Canada. Perils insured include fire, lightning, explosion and smoke; windstorm and hail;
weight of snow, ice and sleet; freezing and frozen pipe damage to installation property; theft, burglary and
attempted theft; vandalism and malicious mischief; collision, overturn and upset of a transporting
conveyance; falling objects and dropped loads; collapse; accidental discharge of water; flood and earth
movement (sublimited); riot and civil commotion; and sudden and accidental physical damage occurring during
the testing or commissioning of systems installed by the Insured.

  Flood and Earth Movement sublimit: $ 500,000 per occurrence / $ 500,000 annual aggregate, applicable to
  Sections 1 and 2 combined, excluding any location in a FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area.

EXCLUSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1.  Faulty workmanship, materials, design or specification - the cost of making good the defective work
      or material itself. Resulting physical damage to other covered property from a Covered Cause of Loss
      is insured.
  2.  Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion, scaling, oxidation, inherent vice and latent
      defect.
  3.  Mechanical, electrical, electronic or hydraulic breakdown or derangement of equipment, including
      compressor, motor, control board and refrigerant circuit failure. Resulting fire, explosion or
      collision damage IS covered.
  4.  Damage to any system caused by its own testing pressure exceeding the manufacturer's rating, or by
      testing performed outside the Insured's normal working hours without an attendant present, or in
      breach of the Water Damage Control Condition below.
  5.  Property after installation is complete and accepted, after the property is put to its intended use,
      or more than ninety (90) days after installation, whichever occurs first.
  6.  Property that is or becomes the subject of a builders risk policy carried by the project owner or
      general contractor, to the extent that policy provides valid and collectible insurance. This policy is
      excess over any such builders risk or installation coverage.
  7.  Mysterious disappearance, unexplained shortage or shortage disclosed on taking inventory.
  8.  Theft from an unlocked and unattended vehicle unless there are visible marks of forced entry, or from
      an open jobsite where the property was not stored in a locked container, secured room or fenced
      compound.
  9.  Dishonest acts of the Insured, its partners, officers or directors (employee theft is covered under
      Coverage I of Section 2 subject to its aggregate limit).
  10. Loss of use, delay, loss of contract, liquidated damages, penalties, soft costs, and all consequential
      loss.
  11. Contractors equipment while being used for demolition, underground mining, pile driving, or lifted or
      suspended in the air except as part of a normal installation operation.
  12. Licensed highway vehicles, trucks, tractors, trailers, service vans and any vehicle subject to motor
      vehicle registration (insure under commercial auto), and aircraft, watercraft and drones.
  13. Property while waterborne.
  14. Mould, fungus, wet rot, dry rot and bacteria, and any resulting remediation.
  15. Pollution and contamination, other than pollutant cleanup necessitated by a covered loss up to
      $ 100,000 annual aggregate.
  16. War, terrorism (TRIA rejected), nuclear hazard, governmental seizure, confiscation and repossession.
  17. Cyber incident, malicious code and electronic data corruption, including building automation and
      controls programming and configuration data.
  18. Refrigerant loss, leakage, contamination or the cost of refrigerant itself, except where lost as a
      direct result of a Covered Cause of Loss, sublimited to $ 50,000 per occurrence.

WATER DAMAGE CONTROL CONDITION (FRM-WTR 555) AND SECURITY CONDITION (FRM-SEC 610)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WATER DAMAGE CONTROL. It is a condition of coverage under Sections 1 and 2 that: (a) hydrostatic and
pressure testing of any piping system installed by the Insured is performed only during the Insured's normal
working hours with a qualified employee present; (b) no system is left charged, pressurised or filled
outside working hours unless the building water supply is isolated or leak detection with automatic shut-off
is installed and operational; (c) a written and signed test record is completed for each system tested; and
(d) the general contractor or building owner is notified in writing before any system is filled or charged.
This condition mirrors the condition in companion policy GL-8804-27153.

SECURITY CONDITION. Copper pipe, fittings, wire, condensing units, compressors, packaged rooftop units and
similar high-theft property must be stored in a locked conex container, a locked job trailer, or a secured
interior room, and must not be left in the open at a jobsite overnight. Service vans must be locked and
tool compartments secured when unattended. Where this condition is not met, the theft deductible is doubled
and the recoverable amount is limited to 50% of the loss.
