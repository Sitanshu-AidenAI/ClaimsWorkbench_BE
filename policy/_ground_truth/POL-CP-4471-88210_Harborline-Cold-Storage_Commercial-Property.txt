SECTION V - COVERED CAUSES OF LOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This policy insures against direct physical loss of or damage to Covered Property at the premises described
in the Declarations from any cause of loss that is not excluded or limited elsewhere in this policy. Perils
expressly contemplated and insured, subject to the deductibles and sublimits shown, include but are not
limited to: fire and lightning; explosion; smoke; windstorm and hail; riot, civil commotion and vandalism;
malicious mischief; aircraft or vehicle impact; sprinkler leakage; accidental discharge or leakage of water
or steam from plumbing, heating, air conditioning or refrigeration systems; theft and burglary; weight of
snow, ice or sleet; collapse as specifically provided in the Additional Coverage - Collapse; and breakage of
glass forming part of the building.

SECTION VI - EXCLUSIONS (summarized; refer to the coverage form for full wording)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. We do not pay for loss or damage caused directly or indirectly by any of the following, regardless of any
   other cause or event that contributes concurrently or in any sequence to the loss:
   1.  Ordinance or law regulating construction, use, repair or demolition, except as provided under the
       Ordinance or Law endorsement attached.
   2.  Earth movement, including earthquake, landslide, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse and volcanic
       action, except ensuing fire or explosion.
   3.  Flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, storm surge, overflow of any body of water, and water that
       backs up or overflows from a sewer, drain or sump, except as written back by endorsement.
   4.  Water below the surface of the ground, including water which exerts pressure on or flows, seeps or
       leaks through foundations, walls, floors, paved surfaces, basements or doors.
   5.  War, warlike action, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, and nuclear hazard.
   6.  Utility services failure originating away from the described premises, except as provided under the
       Off-Premises Utility Service Interruption endorsement.
   7.  Governmental action, including seizure or destruction of property by order of a governmental authority,
       except acts of destruction ordered to prevent the spread of fire.

B. We do not pay for loss or damage caused by or resulting from:
   1.  Wear and tear, deterioration, inherent vice, latent defect, rust, corrosion, decay, hidden or latent
       defect, or any quality in the property that causes it to damage or destroy itself.
   2.  Smog, settling, cracking, shrinking or expansion.
   3.  Nesting or infestation, discharge or release of waste products or secretions by insects, birds,
       rodents or other animals.
   4.  Mechanical breakdown, including rupture or bursting caused by centrifugal force, except as provided
       under the Equipment Breakdown endorsement.
   5.  Continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water, or the presence or condensation of humidity,
       moisture or vapor, that occurs over a period of 14 days or more.
   6.  Dishonest or criminal acts by the Named Insured, any partner, member, officer, manager, employee,
       director, trustee or authorized representative, whether acting alone or in collusion.
   7.  Voluntary parting with any property by the Named Insured or anyone entrusted with the property if
       induced by any fraudulent scheme, trick, device or false pretense.
   8.  Rain, snow, ice or sleet to personal property in the open.
   9.  Errors or omissions in design, specifications, workmanship, repair, construction, renovation,
       remodeling, grading or compaction. However, if such error or omission results in a Covered Cause of
       Loss, we will pay for the loss caused by that Covered Cause of Loss.
   10. Fungus, wet rot, dry rot and bacteria, except as provided under the Limited Fungus Coverage
       endorsement, sublimited to $ 25,000 annual aggregate.
   11. Loss of or damage to property while in the course of construction, erection or fabrication, other than
       ordinary maintenance and repair not exceeding $ 500,000 in project value.
   12. Cyber incident, including unauthorized access to or use of any computer system, and any malicious code
       or similar mechanism, per Endorsement HARBOR-CYB 440 (Cyber Incident Exclusion with Ensuing Fire and
       Explosion Write-Back).

SECTION IX - PROTECTIVE SAFEGUARDS WARRANTY (HARBOR-PROT 610)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

As a condition of this insurance, the insured warrants that the following protective safeguards will be
maintained in complete working order at Premises 001 at all times while this policy is in force:

    P-1  Automatic dry-pipe sprinkler protection throughout the refrigerated envelope, inspected and tested
         quarterly by a licensed contractor, with records retained for three years.
    P-4  Central-station monitored ammonia detection with audible alarm at 25 ppm and automatic plant
         shutdown at 150 ppm.
    P-9  Central-station monitored temperature alarm on each refrigerated room, set at 5 degrees F above the
         room set point, with a 30 minute notification protocol.

Coverage is suspended for any loss occurring while a listed safeguard is impaired or out of service, unless
the insured (a) notified us in writing within 48 hours of becoming aware of the impairment, and (b)
implemented an approved fire watch or equivalent interim protection.

SECTION X - CONDITIONS AND CLAIM REPORTING REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.  DUTIES IN THE EVENT OF LOSS OR DAMAGE. The insured must see that the following are done:
    a.  Notify the police if a law may have been broken.
    b.  Give us prompt notice of the loss or damage, and in no event later than thirty (30) days after the
        insured knew or reasonably should have known of the loss. Notice must include a description of the
        property involved and the time, place and circumstances of the loss.
        Report all losses to:
            Meridian Atlantic Claims Service Center
            claims@meridianatlantic-ins.example  |  24-Hour First Notice: (877) 555-0410
            Mail: P.O. Box 71155, Baltimore, MD 21279-1155
        Reference the policy number shown in the Declarations on all correspondence.
    c.  Take all reasonable steps to protect the Covered Property from further damage, and keep a record of
        expenses necessary to protect the property. We will pay reasonable protective expenses; this does
        not increase the Limit of Insurance.
    d.  Permit us to inspect the property and records proving the loss, and cooperate with the investigation.
    e.  Submit a signed, sworn Proof of Loss within sixty (60) days of our request, containing the
        information we request to investigate the claim.
    f.  Not, except at the insured's own cost, voluntarily make a payment, assume any obligation, or incur
        any expense other than for first aid or emergency mitigation.
2.  APPRAISAL. If we and the insured disagree on the value of the property or the amount of loss, either may
    make written demand for an appraisal of the loss, each party selecting a competent and impartial
    appraiser, with an umpire selected by the two appraisers.
3.  LOSS PAYMENT. We will pay for covered loss within thirty (30) days after we receive the sworn Proof of
    Loss, provided the amount of loss has been agreed or an appraisal award has been made.
4.  LEGAL ACTION AGAINST US. No one may bring a legal action against us unless there has been full compliance
    with the policy terms and the action is brought within two (2) years after the date on which the direct
    physical loss or damage occurred.
5.  OTHER INSURANCE. This insurance is excess over any other insurance covering the same loss, whether
    collectible or not, except insurance written specifically as excess of the limits of this policy.
6.  VACANCY. If the building has been vacant for more than sixty (60) consecutive days before a loss, we will
    not pay for loss caused by vandalism, sprinkler leakage (unless the system was protected against
    freezing), building glass breakage, water damage, theft or attempted theft; and we will reduce the amount
    we would otherwise pay for any other covered loss by 15%.
7.  SUBROGATION. If any person or organization to or for whom we make payment has rights to recover damages
    from another, those rights are transferred to us. The insured may waive those rights in writing prior to
    loss without prejudicing this insurance.
8.  PREMIUM. Annual premium $ 486,250. Terrorism premium $ 0 (coverage rejected). Minimum earned premium 25%.
