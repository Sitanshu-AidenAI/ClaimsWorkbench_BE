COVERED CAUSES OF LOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Risks of direct physical loss of or damage to covered property, except as excluded. Perils insured include:
fire, lightning, explosion and smoke; windstorm, hail, haboob, blowing sand and dust; theft, burglary,
robbery and attempted theft; vandalism and malicious mischief; collision, overturn, upset, derailment and
collapse of a bridge or culvert while the equipment is being transported; collision of the equipment itself
while being operated or moved under its own power at a jobsite; falling objects; collapse of a structure,
trench, embankment or excavation in which the equipment is located; landslide and mudslide; flood, rising
water and flash flood while the equipment is outside a Special Flood Hazard Area; earthquake; freezing;
riot and civil commotion; sinking, submersion and washout; damage arising from the failure of rigging or
lifting gear; and the collapse or overturn of a crane, boom lift or telehandler.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXCLUSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This policy does not cover:
  1.  Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion, oxidation, cavitation, erosion, dry rot,
      inherent vice, latent defect, or the gradual ingress of dust, sand or moisture.
  2.  Mechanical, electrical, electronic or hydraulic breakdown, failure or derangement, including engine,
      transmission, final drive, pump, motor, controller and computer failure. However, resulting damage
      from an ensuing fire, explosion or collision IS covered.
  3.  Damage to tyres, tracks, undercarriage, cutting edges, ground engaging tools, teeth, augers, drill
      bits, hoses, belts and other consumable or wear items, unless damaged in the same occurrence as other
      insured damage to the item, or unless the loss is caused by fire, theft, vandalism or collision.
  4.  Loss caused by or resulting from overload, overstress, exceeding the manufacturer's rated capacity,
      or use of the equipment beyond its designed purpose or load chart.
  5.  Loss caused by improper rigging, lifting or securement, unless a certified rigger or a qualified
      person as defined by OSHA 1926 Subpart CC directed the lift and a written lift plan was prepared for
      any lift exceeding 75% of the rated capacity, or any multi-crane, blind or critical lift.
  6.  Loss to any crane, boom lift or aerial device operated by a person not holding a current NCCCO or
      equivalent certification for that equipment class.
  7.  Mysterious disappearance, unexplained loss, or shortage disclosed by taking inventory.
  8.  Theft from an unlocked and unattended vehicle, trailer or container, or from an open jobsite where the
      equipment was not immobilised, secured or fenced, unless there are visible marks of forced entry.
  9.  Loss caused by any dishonest or criminal act of the insured, any partner, officer or director, other
      than employee theft covered under Coverage N.
  10. Voluntary parting with property induced by a fraudulent scheme, trick, false pretense or false
      documentation, including fraudulent rental, hire or sale.
  11. Loss to property while waterborne except as provided under Coverage I.
  12. Loss to property leased or rented to others by the insured, unless the insured retains operational
      control and the property remains at a site under the insured's supervision.
  13. Any loss of use, loss of contract, delay, liquidated damages, loss of market or consequential loss,
      except as provided under Coverages E and M.
  14. Loss caused by unlicensed, unauthorised or untrained operation of the equipment.
  15. Property while being used in any underground mining operation, tunnelling below 50 feet, marine
      salvage, or on any body of water other than as provided under Coverage I.
  16. Property in a Special Flood Hazard Area (FEMA Zone A, AE, AO, AH, V or VE) at the time of a flood
      loss, and any loss caused by flood where the equipment was left in a wash, arroyo, drainage channel
      or below the ordinary high water mark.
  17. Automobiles, trucks, tractors, trailers, licensed motor vehicles and any equipment subject to motor
      vehicle registration and designed principally for highway use. (Insure under a commercial auto
      policy.)
  18. Watercraft, aircraft, unmanned aerial vehicles and drones.
  19. Loss to property that is a permanent part of a building or structure, or that is intended to become a
      permanent part of the insured's construction project. (Insure under a builders risk or installation
      floater.)
  20. War, terrorism (unless elected), nuclear hazard, governmental seizure, confiscation and repossession.
  21. Cyber incident, malicious code and loss of electronic data, including telematics and machine control
      data (End. DBS-CYB 501).

JOBSITE AND YARD SECURITY WARRANTY (DBS-SEC 610) AND TELEMATICS CONDITION (DBS-TELE 445)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECURITY WARRANTY. The Insured warrants that: (a) all yard locations are fenced to a minimum of six feet,
gated and locked outside working hours, illuminated, and monitored by a recorded camera system; (b) at
jobsites, all equipment with a scheduled value of $ 100,000 or more is immobilised outside working hours by
removal of the ignition key and engagement of a hydraulic lock-out, wheel lock, or by parking within a
secured compound; (c) keys are not left in or on any machine; (d) buckets and attachments are lowered to
the ground and boom equipment is retracted and stowed at the end of each shift; and (e) fuel and tool
trailers and conex containers are padlocked with a shrouded or hidden-shackle lock.

TELEMATICS CONDITION. A 12.5% premium credit has been applied on the condition that active GPS/telematics
tracking is installed, powered and monitored on every scheduled item with a limit of $ 100,000 or more.
The Insured must supply the tracking unit identifier for each such item and must report any tracking unit
failure exceeding fourteen (14) days. Where a theft loss occurs on an item whose tracker was disabled,
removed or non-functional for more than fourteen (14) days before the loss, the applicable deductible is
doubled.
