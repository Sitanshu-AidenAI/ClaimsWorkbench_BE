COVERED CAUSES OF LOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All risks of direct physical loss of or damage to insured project property, except as excluded. Perils
specifically contemplated for this project include: fire, lightning and lightning-induced surge; explosion
and arc flash; windstorm, hail, microburst, haboob and blowing dust and sand; monsoon rain and water
intrusion into an incomplete envelope; theft and burglary; vandalism and malicious mischief; vehicle and
aircraft impact; dropped loads, crane failure, rigging failure and damage during setting of heavy
equipment; collapse during erection; accidental discharge of water from temporary or permanent piping;
smoke; sudden and accidental physical damage occurring during testing, energisation, load bank testing and
integrated systems testing, as endorsed.

EXCLUSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1.  The cost of making good faulty or defective design, plan, specification, materials or workmanship.
      Resulting damage from a Covered Cause of Loss to other insured property is covered. (LEG 2/96,
      End. DBS-DEF 305.)
  2.  Loss to any equipment, component or system caused by its own electrical or mechanical breakdown,
      derangement, or failure to function, except where such breakdown results in fire, explosion or other
      physical damage to separate property, and except to the extent provided by the Testing, Commissioning
      and Energisation endorsement, subject to the $ 500,000 deductible.
  3.  Damage arising from operation of the completed facility, or from any part of the facility placed into
      commercial service or beneficial use, without our prior written Partial Occupancy / Beneficial Use
      endorsement.
  4.  Delay in completion, loss of use, loss of revenue, business interruption, liquidated damages,
      penalties, and all consequential loss. (Delay in Start-Up coverage NOT purchased.)
  5.  Contractors' and subcontractors' tools, cranes, lifts, mobile and rolling equipment, vehicles and
      trailers not destined to become a permanent part of the project.
  6.  Flood, surface water, mudflow, wash, arroyo overflow and water that backs up from a sewer or drain.
  7.  Earth movement, earthquake, subsidence, expansive soils, collapsible soils, settlement and fissuring,
      including damage arising from the well-documented earth fissure hazard in the region.
  8.  Wear and tear, deterioration, corrosion, oxidation, rust, insulation degradation, cavitation, and
      damage from dust or sand ingress that occurs gradually rather than from a discrete storm event.
  9.  Mysterious disappearance, unexplained shortage and inventory shortage.
  10. Dishonest, fraudulent or criminal acts by any insured, employee, or subcontractor of any tier.
  11. Loss of or damage to Data Hall A, the campus substation feeder beyond the point of interconnection,
      and all other existing structures, equipment and improvements on the campus.
  12. Loss caused by or resulting from the failure to protect exposed openings, materials or equipment prior
      to a forecast monsoon, haboob or high-wind event, or failure to comply with the Site Protection
      Warranty.
  13. Loss to lithium-ion battery cells, modules or cabinets caused by internal thermal runaway,
      overcharging or manufacturing defect; ensuing fire damage to other insured property is covered
      (End. DBS-LI 530, Lithium-Ion Energy Storage Limitation).
  14. Cyber incident, malicious code, unauthorised access to control or building management systems, and
      loss of or corruption to electronic data (End. DBS-CYB 501); ensuing fire and explosion are written
      back.
  15. War, terrorism (unless TRIA elected), nuclear reaction and radioactive contamination.
  16. Any loss occurring after final acceptance, after issuance of the certificate of occupancy for the
      insured building, or after policy expiry, whichever occurs first.

SITE PROTECTION AND MONSOON PREPAREDNESS WARRANTY (DBS-PROT 610)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Insureds warrant that, as a condition precedent to coverage:
  (a)  The site is enclosed by a minimum eight-foot fence with anti-climb fabric and controlled access
       gates, monitored by a recorded camera system with 24/7 off-site monitoring, and patrolled by a
       licensed guard between 18:00 and 06:00 daily;
  (b)  All electrical equipment delivered to site is stored energised with strip heaters, or in a
       conditioned enclosure, or with desiccant and humidity indicators inspected weekly and logged;
  (c)  Switchgear, transformers, UPS modules and generator sets are not left in open storage; equipment
       awaiting installation is placed on the pad, sealed and space-heated within seven (7) days of
       delivery, or stored at the declared off-site facility;
  (d)  A written Monsoon Preparedness Plan is maintained from June 15 to September 30 requiring, upon a
       National Weather Service severe thunderstorm, dust storm or flash flood watch: securing of loose
       materials and debris, closing and sealing of building openings, covering of equipment, removal of
       crane loads, and lowering of boom and mast equipment;
  (d)  A hot work permit programme meeting NFPA 51B and jobsite fire prevention per NFPA 241 are enforced.
