EXCLUSIONS, WARRANTIES AND CONDITIONS SPECIFIC TO ROOFING OPERATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In addition to the standard Coverage A exclusions, the following apply:

  1.  HOT WORK, TORCH AND KETTLE WARRANTY (End. TUE-HOT 430). It is warranted that the insured will not
      perform any torch-applied, open flame, kettle, hot air welding above 900 degrees F, or heat-gun
      operation on any roof unless:
        (a)  a written hot work permit is issued for each work area each day;
        (b)  a dedicated fire watch, separate from the operator, is posted during the work and for a
             minimum of two (2) hours after the work stops, with a documented final check thirty (30)
             minutes later;
        (c)  a minimum of two 20-lb ABC extinguishers and a charged water source are within 25 feet;
        (d)  combustible materials are removed or covered with listed fire-resistant blankets within 35
             feet; and
        (e)  the daily permit and fire watch log are retained for three (3) years.
      Failure to comply excludes any loss caused by or resulting from that operation.

  2.  OPEN ROOF AND WEATHER PROTECTION CONDITION (End. TUE-OPEN 445). It is a condition of coverage for
      water damage that the insured, at the end of each work period and before any forecast precipitation:
        (a)  installs a temporary watertight covering over all open or removed areas, mechanically fastened
             and lapped, and seals all penetrations and terminations;
        (b)  does not remove more roof surface in any one day than can be dried in before the end of that
             work period;
        (c)  monitors the National Weather Service forecast for the work location each morning and does not
             open a roof where the probability of precipitation exceeds 40% within the work period; and
        (d)  documents daily tear-off and dry-in photographs.
      Water damage arising from a failure to comply is excluded. Where the condition is met, water damage
      is covered subject to the sublimit stated above.

  3.  PROFESSIONAL SERVICES EXCLUSION (TUE-PROF 415), including roof design, load calculations, moisture
      surveys, infrared scans and consulting reports issued for a fee.
  4.  ABSOLUTE MOULD, FUNGI AND MICROBIAL MATTER EXCLUSION (TUE-MOLD 420).
  5.  RESIDENTIAL RE-ROOFING EXCLUSION (TUE-RES 425) - no coverage for work on single family dwellings,
      duplexes, townhomes or condominium units of three stories or fewer.
  6.  SUBCONTRACTED WORK EXCLUSION (TUE-SUBK 435) - no coverage for bodily injury or property damage arising
      out of work performed by any subcontractor of the insured unless (a) a written subcontract was in
      place, (b) a certificate evidencing CGL limits of at least $ 1,000,000 each occurrence naming the
      insured as an additional insured for ongoing and completed operations was obtained before the work
      began, and (c) the subcontractor's policy contains no roofing exclusion.
  7.  ASBESTOS, SILICA AND LEAD EXCLUSION (TUE-SIL 440), including exposure arising from tear-off of
      existing built-up roofing containing asbestos.
  8.  HEIGHT LIMITATION (TUE-HGT 450) - no coverage for operations on any structure exceeding six (6)
      stories or seventy-five (75) feet in height without prior written approval endorsed to this policy.
  9.  EARTH MOVEMENT, COMMUNICABLE DISEASE, CYBER, EMPLOYMENT PRACTICES, and PUNITIVE DAMAGES where
      insurance is prohibited by law.
  10. DAMAGE TO YOUR WORK - the roof system installed by the insured, subject to the standard subcontractor
      exception within the products-completed operations hazard.

    forty-eight (48) hours, of: any fire occurring at or within 24 hours after any hot work operation; any
    water intrusion into a building on which the insured has performed or is performing work; any fall from
    height, dropped object or injury to a person who is not an employee; and any damage to rooftop
    equipment, solar arrays or third-party property.

        claims@tidewaterexchange.example   |   (888) 555-0611 (24 hour)
        After-hours emergency mitigation referral: (888) 555-0612
2.  MITIGATION AND MANAGED REPAIR. Upon a water intrusion event the insured must arrange immediate
    temporary dry-in and must notify the building owner. Emergency mitigation up to $ 10,000 may be
    incurred without our prior approval; larger scopes require our authorisation or use of a vendor from our
    approved network.
3.  RECORDS TO BE PRODUCED. On our request the insured must produce: the daily hot work permit and fire
    watch log; the daily tear-off and dry-in photographs; the crew sign-in sheets; the weather forecast
    record for the work location; the subcontract and certificate of insurance for any subcontractor
    involved; and the signed work order or contract with the building owner.
4.  COOPERATION AND VOLUNTARY PAYMENTS. The insured must cooperate in the defense and must not make any
    payment, admit liability or assume any obligation without our consent, except for first aid.
5.  OTHER INSURANCE. This insurance is excess over any builders risk, installation floater or property
    insurance covering the work, and over any coverage available to the insured as an additional insured,
    except where a written contract executed prior to loss requires this policy to respond on a primary and
    non-contributory basis.
6.  PREMIUM AUDIT. Annual, based on payroll. Certified payroll records must be retained for three years.
7.  LEGAL ACTION. No action may be brought against us until there has been full compliance with all terms,
    and not until the obligation of the insured has been determined by judgment or by written agreement.
