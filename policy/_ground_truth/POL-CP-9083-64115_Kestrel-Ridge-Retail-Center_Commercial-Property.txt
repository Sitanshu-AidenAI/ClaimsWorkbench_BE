COVERED CAUSES OF LOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Risks of direct physical loss to Covered Property unless the loss is excluded or limited. Perils
contemplated include fire, lightning, windstorm, hail, explosion, smoke, aircraft or vehicle impact
(including impact by an automobile driven into or against a storefront or building wall), riot and civil
commotion, vandalism, malicious mischief, sprinkler leakage, accidental discharge of water from plumbing,
heating, cooling, sprinkler or appliance systems, theft and burglary, weight of snow, ice or sleet, falling
objects, volcanic action, and collapse to the extent provided by the Additional Coverage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXCLUSIONS (abridged - see coverage form for full text)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1.  Flood, surface water, mudflow, and water that backs up from a sewer, drain or sump (backup partially
      written back at the sublimit shown).
  2.  Earth movement, including earthquake and volcanic eruption, except as provided by End. NSS-EQ 350.
  3.  Ordinance or law, except as provided by the Ordinance or Law coverage shown.
  4.  Wear and tear, deterioration, rust, corrosion, hidden or latent defect, settling, cracking, shrinking
      or expansion.
  5.  Mechanical breakdown, except as provided by the Equipment Breakdown coverage.
  6.  Continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water over a period of 14 days or more.
  7.  Fungus, wet or dry rot, mold and bacteria, except limited coverage of $ 15,000 annual aggregate
      resulting from a covered water loss (End. NSS-FUN 140).
  8.  Faulty, inadequate or defective design, workmanship, construction, materials or maintenance; ensuing
      loss from a Covered Cause of Loss is covered.
  9.  Dishonest or criminal acts by the insured, its partners, employees, managers or authorised
      representatives, or by any tenant.
  10. Loss of or damage to property in the course of construction of a new building; alterations and
      additions to existing insured buildings are covered to $ 500,000 of contract value.
  11. Theft of, or damage to, tenant trade fixtures, stock or personal property; such property is the
      responsibility of the tenant under the lease.
  12. War, terrorism (unless elected), nuclear hazard, governmental seizure, and communicable disease.
  13. Damage to tenant cooking equipment, grease ducts and hoods from any grease-fuelled fire originating
      within a Type I hood system, except where the automatic wet chemical suppression system discharged as
      designed (End. NSS-COOK 160).

CONDITIONS AND CLAIM REPORTING REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.  DUTIES IN THE EVENT OF LOSS. The insured must (a) notify the police where a law may have been broken;
    (b) give us prompt notice of the loss, including a description of the property involved; (c) as soon as
    possible, give a description of how, when and where the loss occurred; (d) take all reasonable steps to
    protect the Covered Property from further damage and keep records of the expense; (e) permit us to
    inspect and take samples of damaged and undamaged property; (f) submit to examination under oath if
    requested; and (g) send us a signed, sworn Proof of Loss within sixty (60) days of our request.
    First notice of loss:  claims@northshorespecialty.example  |  (866) 555-0221 (24 hour)
    Claims mailing address: P.O. Box 8840, Boise, ID 83707
2.  EMERGENCY MITIGATION. The insured may engage a qualified restoration contractor for emergency board-up,
    water extraction, temporary roof cover or fencing without prior approval, provided (a) the cost does not
    exceed $ 15,000, (b) we are notified within 24 hours, and (c) photographs of the affected areas are
    taken before mitigation begins.
3.  TENANT INTERESTS. This policy does not insure the personal property, stock, trade fixtures or
    improvements installed at a tenant's expense, except where the lease makes the landlord responsible.
4.  VACANCY PERMIT. Notwithstanding the Vacancy Condition, coverage for vandalism, sprinkler leakage, water
    damage, theft and glass breakage is continued for Suites 112 and 116 of Building 01 to 07/01/2025, on
    condition that the suites are secured, alarmed, heated to a minimum of 55 degrees F, and inspected not
    less than weekly with a written log maintained.
5.  APPRAISAL, SUBROGATION and other conditions apply as stated in the coverage form.
6.  SUIT LIMITATION. Any legal action must be brought within two (2) years of the date of loss.
7.  PREMIUM. Annual premium $ 168,940. Terrorism premium $ 0 (rejected).
