PERILS INSURED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

All risks of direct physical loss of or damage to insured property occurring during the policy period, except
as excluded. Perils specifically contemplated include fire, lightning, explosion (including combustion
explosion in the boiler house subject to the boiler deductible), windstorm, hail, tornado, riot, strike and
civil commotion, vandalism and malicious mischief, aircraft and vehicle impact, sprinkler leakage, water
damage from the accidental discharge of any plumbing, process, steam or fire protection system, collapse,
smoke, theft and burglary, and machinery breakdown as endorsed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXCLUSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This policy does not insure against:
  1.  Loss arising from a defect, fault or error in design, plan, specification, materials or workmanship;
      however, resulting physical damage from an insured peril is covered.
  2.  Wear, tear, gradual deterioration, depletion, rust, corrosion, erosion, cavitation, embrittlement,
      inherent vice or latent defect.
  3.  Shrinkage, evaporation, loss of weight, change in flavour, texture, colour or finish, unless resulting
      from an insured peril.
  4.  Loss of or damage to property undergoing any process of heating, drying, curing, dyeing or thermal
      treatment, if caused by that process; however, resulting fire damage to other property is covered.
  5.  Faulty or inadequate maintenance, including failure to maintain fire protection equipment.
  6.  Settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging or expansion of foundations, floors, walls, roofs or pavements.
  7.  Mysterious disappearance, unexplained loss, or shortage disclosed on taking inventory.
  8.  Dishonest or fraudulent acts of any insured, employee, or any person to whom property is entrusted.
  9.  Loss or damage caused by or resulting from any electronic data corruption, computer virus, ransomware,
      denial of service, or unauthorised network access (Cyber Exclusion ACR-CYB 501), except ensuing
      physical damage from fire, explosion or sprinkler leakage.
  10. Contamination, including but not limited to pollution, asbestos, lead, PFAS and per- and
      polyfluoroalkyl substances (ACR-PFAS 512), and any cost of testing, monitoring or remediation.
  11. Loss of market, loss of use, delay, or any other consequential loss except as expressly insured under
      the Time Element sections.
  12. Communicable disease, pandemic, epidemic or fear thereof (ACR-CD 522).
  13. War, terrorism (unless TRIA elected), nuclear reaction, radiation or radioactive contamination.
  14. Any loss occurring while the insured location has been unoccupied or idle for more than ninety (90)
      consecutive days, unless declared to and accepted by the Company in writing.
  15. Seepage or contamination that occurs over a period of more than 30 days.

PROTECTIVE SAFEGUARDS AND HOT WORK WARRANTY (ACR-PROT 601)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

It is warranted by the Insured that:
  (a)  All automatic sprinkler systems, the diesel fire pump and the private fire main at LOC 1 will be
       maintained in complete working order and inspected in accordance with NFPA 25;
  (b)  A written hot work permit system meeting NFPA 51B will be enforced at all locations, including a
       35-foot clearance, assigned fire watch during the work and for a minimum of sixty (60) minutes after
       the work ends, and a documented final check thirty (30) minutes thereafter;
  (c)  Lint and fibre accumulation in the Weave Room and in all dust collection ductwork will be removed on
       a documented schedule not less frequently than weekly;
  (d)  Any impairment of fire protection exceeding eight (8) hours will be reported to the Company and to the
       central station within twenty-four (24) hours.
Failure to comply with any of the above voids coverage for loss arising from or contributed to by such
failure.

CLAIM CONDITIONS AND REPORTING REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.  NOTICE. The Insured shall report to the Company, as soon as practicable and in any event within
    forty-eight (48) hours of discovery, any loss or occurrence that may reasonably be expected to exceed
    fifty percent (50%) of the applicable deductible. Notice shall be given to:
        Allegheny Commercial Risk - Complex Property Claims
        firstnotice@alleghenycommercialrisk.example   |   (888) 555-0177 (24 hour)
        Claims Manager, Southeast Region: T. Okonkwo-Brandt
2.  PRESERVATION OF EVIDENCE. The Insured shall preserve all damaged and undamaged property, machinery,
    control records and electronic logs relevant to the loss, and shall not dispose of any such property
    without the Company's written consent, until the Company or its representatives have had a reasonable
    opportunity to inspect.
3.  MITIGATION. The Insured shall take all reasonable and necessary steps to protect insured property from
    further damage and to resume operations. Reasonable expenses so incurred are payable under Extra Expense.
4.  PROOF OF LOSS. A signed and sworn statement of loss shall be filed within ninety (90) days of the date
    of loss unless extended in writing by the Company.
5.  PARTIAL PAYMENTS. Where liability is not in dispute, the Company will advance partial payments on account
    within thirty (30) days of the Insured's written request supported by documentation.
6.  SUIT LIMITATION. No suit shall be maintainable unless commenced within twenty-four (24) months of the
    date of the physical loss or damage.
7.  PREMIUM. Annual premium $ 1,268,400, plus South Carolina surplus lines tax 6% and stamping fee 0.1%.
    Minimum earned premium at inception: 25%.
