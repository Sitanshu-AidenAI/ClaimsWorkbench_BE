EXCLUSIONS (abridged)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We will not pay for loss or damage caused directly or indirectly by:
  1.  Flood, surface water, waves, storm surge, or water that backs up from a sewer or drain (backup written
      back at the sublimit shown), or water under the ground surface pressing on foundations or floors.
  2.  Earth movement, other than as provided by the Earthquake endorsement, which insures shock and ensuing
      fire only and excludes damage to swimming pools, retaining walls, fences and paved surfaces.
  3.  Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, settling, cracking, shrinking, bulging or expansion of pavements,
      foundations, walls, floors, roofs or ceilings.
  4.  Marring, scratching, and cosmetic damage to roof surfacing that does not compromise the water-shedding
      function of the roof (End. HAB-COS 315, Cosmetic Damage Exclusion - Roof Surfacing).
  5.  Faulty, inadequate or defective design, workmanship, repair, construction, materials or maintenance;
      however, ensuing loss from a Covered Cause of Loss is insured.
  6.  Continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water, or presence of humidity or condensation, occurring
      over a period of 14 days or more, whether or not the insured was aware of it.
  7.  Fungus, wet rot, dry rot, bacteria, mold or mildew, except to the limited extent provided by
      Endorsement HAB-FUN 140.
  8.  Theft or dishonest acts by any insured, any tenant, or any person to whom the property is entrusted.
  9.  Damage caused by animals, birds, vermin, rodents, insects or domestic pets.
  10. Loss to property while in the course of construction of a new building or structure. Renovation of an
      existing insured building is covered up to $ 500,000 of contract value; larger projects require a
      separate builder's risk policy.
  11. War, terrorism (unless TRIA coverage is accepted), nuclear hazard and governmental action.
  12. Communicable disease, virus and bacteria - loss of use, contamination, decontamination and business
      interruption arising therefrom (End. HAB-CD 410).

    sent to:
        Cascadia Mutual Claims Intake
        Email: newloss@cascadiamutual.example     Phone: (800) 555-0288 (24 hour)
        Portal: claims.cascadiamutual.example

6.  SUIT LIMITATION. Any action must be commenced within two (2) years after the date of loss.
